Daniel Caesar is a Grammy-winning Canadian R&B singer -songwriter known for his soulful, gospel-infused sound and introspective lyrics, rising from indie roots with EPs like Praise Break to mainstream success with the acclaimed album Freudian (2017) and hits like "Best Part," earning acclaim for exploring love, faith, and identity. He's celebrated for his smooth vocals and rich instrumentation, blending contemporary R&B with neo-soul and alternative elements, solidifying his status as a major force in modern R&B.
